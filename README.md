WealthPlanner User Manual

Welcome to WealthPlanner, a minimalist and powerful tool designed to help you track your net worth, manage your assets and liabilities, and project your future financial growth.

1. Getting Started

WealthPlanner is a browser-based application that stores your data locally or syncs it with your preferred cloud storage.

Interface Navigation

Sidebar (Menu): On desktop, the sidebar is fixed to the left. On mobile, tap the ☰ (Menu) icon in the top-left to open it.

Top Bar: Contains quick actions including Settings (⚙️), CSV Export (📥), and Reset Data.

2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of your financial health.

Net Worth: Your Total Assets minus Total Liabilities.

Total Assets: The combined value of everything you own (Cash, Stocks, Real Estate, etc.).

Total Liabilities: The combined value of everything you owe (Loans, Debts).

Liquid Net Worth: The amount of your net worth that is "liquid" (easily accessible cash or near-cash).

Liquidity Ratio: Shows what percentage of your total wealth is immediately accessible.

Allocation Chart: A visual breakdown of how your wealth is distributed across different categories.

3. Managing Your Portfolio

Navigate to the Portfolio tab to add, edit, or remove financial items.

Adding an Item

Enter the Item Name (e.g., "Main Savings" or "Home Loan").

Enter the Total Value.

Set the Annual Interest Rate (used for future projections).

Select the Type (Asset or Liability) and the Category.

Liquidity Settings: - Use % Liquid to indicate what portion is accessible (e.g., 100% for Cash, 0% for Real Estate).

Alternatively, enter an Absolute Liquid value.

Click Add Item.

Editing and Deleting

Edit: Click the pencil icon on any item to load its details back into the form. Update the fields and click "Update Item."

Delete: Click the red trash can icon to permanently remove an item from your portfolio.

4. Financial Projections

The Projections tab calculates where you will be in 10 years based on your current assets, liabilities, and their respective annual interest rates.

Projection Chart: A line graph showing the growth curve of your Net Worth.

Projection Table: A year-by-year breakdown of your estimated value and the specific growth (compounded interest) earned each year.

5. Monthly Interest Tracking

The Monthly Interest tab provides a specific view of your "Passive Income" or "Interest Expense."

It calculates the estimated monthly earnings from your assets (like Savings accounts or Stocks) and the monthly cost of your liabilities (like Loan interest).

6. Settings & Customization

Click the ⚙️ icon in the top bar to access the Settings Modal.

Themes

WealthPlanner includes several visual themes to suit your style:

Default Dark / Midnight Purple / Cyberpunk: High-contrast dark modes.

Light Minimalist / Nordic Frost: Clean, bright interfaces.

Coffee & Sepia / Forest Glow: Warm and natural palettes.

Data Management

Manual Sync: If configured, use this to push your local data to your connected Google Sheet.

Export to CSV: Download a spreadsheet-compatible file (.csv) of your current portfolio for external record-keeping.

Reset Data: Warning! This will clear all items from your current session. Use this only if you want to start your financial planning from scratch.

7. Pro Tips

Accuracy Matters: For the most accurate projections, update your "Annual Interest Rate" for each item based on current market trends or your bank's APY.

Liquidity Tracking: Use the liquidity feature to ensure you have enough "Emergency Fund" (Liquid Net Worth) to cover unexpected expenses.

Privacy: Since WealthPlanner handles sensitive financial data, ensure you are using it on a secure, private device.
